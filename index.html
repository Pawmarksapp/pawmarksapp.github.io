Pawmarks

Bookmark artists. Keep collections. Never lose a favourite.

Pawmarks is a free Chrome extension for keeping track of the artists you follow on image boards.

  • Bookmark artists and save posts into collections
  • A feed of new uploads from artists you follow
  • Lightbox, slideshow, stats and six looks
  • Automatic local backups — your data never leaves your computer

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. An optional one-time Pawmarks Plus unlock raises bulk-download limits and adds unlimited collections.
